# ep05 — Wi-Fi: script for review (stage 2)

**Rule (rule bank #2):** In this world, your Wi-Fi gets stronger the nicer you are.
**Hook text on screen:** In this world, NICE = Wi-Fi 📶
**Look:** Roblox style, `sun` mood, "Numbers" bed. **Length:** 241 words ≈ 60 s at our voice pace.

| Beat | Time | Lines |

| 7 Twist | 0:52 | So I walked to the tower to ask why. The repairman checked my phone and laughed. My Wi-Fi wasn't zero. It was the strongest in town. I was just giving every bar away. To Grandma's whole street. Three hundred and twelve phones. When I was six, I told the tower one thing. Give my Wi-Fi to Grandma. |
| 8 Button | 0:57 | My phone still says Searching. But Grandma just sent me forty-seven heart emojis. In 4K. |

- **Callback:** "Searching" (0:27) comes back in the last line.
- **Twist hidden in plain sight:** "Her street always has amazing Wi-Fi, though." The rule was always true: he's the nicest kid in town.
- **Comment bait:** babies have the fastest internet but can't read; "my Wi-Fi would be dead 💀"; "what if you're only fake nice?"

## Option B — nice to yourself: moral ending

Same until beat 3, where Max says **"I tell myself I'm the worst kid in town."** instead of "Everyone thinks…".
From the tower on:

> The repairman checked my phone and sighed. The tower counts how nice you are to everyone. Including yourself.
> Every day, I called myself the worst. So I said one nice thing about me. Five bars.
> My phone stopped saying Searching. And Grandma picked up on the first ring.

Softer and more emotional, but the "Grandma's street has amazing Wi-Fi" clue is lost, and it's less funny.
